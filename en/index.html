FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an intensive course?

Five 60-minute lessons a week (Mon–Fri), so 20 lessons a month. For example, B1 is 60 lessons, about 3 months. Schools that meet 3 times a week usually take 5–6 months for the same level.

What is the minimum age?

10 years. We teach school students, teenagers, university students and adults.

How big are the groups?

6–8 students of the same level, so the teacher has time for everyone.

Is the placement test free?

Yes. Take the mini test on this site, or text “TEST” on WhatsApp to +7 707 845 73 83 and the administrator will send you the full test.

When should I start IELTS prep?

The school recommends starting after B2 (Intermediate), when a high band is realistic. Preparation usually takes 2–3 months.

How do I pay? Can I pay in instalments?

By Kaspi transfer to the school’s number +7 707 845 73 83 (include the student’s full name). You can pay 50% after the trial lesson and 50% a week later.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If your plans change, the school refunds unused lessons to your Kaspi card with no penalty. For example: you paid 40,000 ₸ and attended 10 of 20 lessons — you get 20,000 ₸ back.

Can I miss lessons?

You can miss or freeze up to 3 of your 20 monthly lessons so you don’t fall behind the group.

Which textbooks do you use?

English File by Oxford University Press. A textbook for one level costs 8,000 ₸ and is available at the school.
